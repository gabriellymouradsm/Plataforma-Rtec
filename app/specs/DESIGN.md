## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-looking union of ecological responsibility, municipal circular economy, and enterprise logistics. Conceived primarily as an intuitive, high-performance Progressive Web App (PWA), it prioritizes field operational agility and citizen trust.

### Brand Narrative & Values
- **Precision Traceability:** Every electronic and recyclable artifact has an audited journey. Visual cues emphasize transparency, verified milestones, and metric rigor.
- **Vibrant Ecology:** Replaces dull, muted earthy tones with crisp, energetic chlorophyll greens paired with mineral slate, conveying technological modernism rather than passive conservation.
- **Institutional Assurance:** Grounded in civic reliability for municipal territories, delivering clear institutional legitimacy without bureaucratic friction.

### Visual Movement: Eco-Modern Fluidity
The visual language merges **Modern Corporate Utility** with **Tactile Glassmorphism & Soft Tonal Surfaces**. The interface uses clean white and cool off-white containers, accented by ambient, emerald-tinted drop shadows, generous touch ergonomics (minimum 48px hit areas for field operators), rounded organic geometries, and fluid ecological headers.

## Colors

The palette balances environmental impact with structural contrast, ensuring WCAG 2.1 AA legibility under direct outdoor sunlight for mobile field users.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#16a34a`):** Forest Emerald. Main brand color, active interactive triggers, primary floating action bars, and confirmed operational nodes.
- **Primary Dark (`#15803d`):** Deep Forest. Focus states, solid active tabs, and gradient terminal stops for top mobile app bars.
- **Primary Light (`#22c55e`):** Fresh Shoot. Gradient origin stops, metric trend indicators, and active toggle markers.
- **Secondary / Eco Accent (`#10b981`):** Vivid Jade. Micro-badges, validation seals, and zero-waste milestone counters.
- **Tertiary / Informative (`#0284c7`):** Logistics Cobalt. Routing guidance, manifest numbers, manifest metadata, and informational dialogs.
- **Warning / Triagem (`#f59e0b`):** Amber Resin. Inspection alerts, battery/hazardous sorting protocols, and queue states.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#0f172a`):** Deep Slate. Headers, titles, critical data figures.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#64748b`):** Muted Slate. Metadata labels, supporting captions, input placeholders.
- **Surface Canvas (`#f8fafc`):** Atmospheric Pearl. Outer screen backdrops.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#f1f5f9`):** Light Pebble. Segmented bar tracks, badge backing, card divider lines.
- **Surface Elevated (`#ffffff`):** Pure White. Interactive actionable cards, bottom sheets, form controls.

### Gradients
- **Header Flow:** Linear gradient at 135 degrees from `#15803d` down to `#22c55e`. Applied strictly across primary top banners, PWA hero stat summaries, and batch dispatch sheets.

## Typography

Typography balances technical, metric-driven credibility with immediate legibility.

- **Display & Headings (Space Grotesk):** Provides a clean, slightly technical, forward-looking aesthetic for metrics, impact tonnage, and operational section titles. Numeric figures use tabular numbers to align kilogram weights, manifest IDs, and collection counts cleanly.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers welcoming, ultra-legible clarity on mobile displays. Its open apertures prevent character confusion in low-light collection points or glare-heavy outdoor logistics conditions.

## Layout & Spacing

This mobile-first architecture is calibrated around safe areas, single-hand thumb navigation, and fluid vertical rhythm.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Mobile PWA Standard (Base):** Single column fluid canvas bounded by `1rem` outer margins and dynamic iOS/Android safe area insets at top and bottom. Maximum content width is pegged at `480px` centered on larger displays, preventing awkward horizontal dispersion.
- **Component Padding Rhythm:** Form inputs and card structures systematically use `space-md` (`1rem`) internally; content clusters separate with `space-sm` (`0.5rem`), while discrete task cards separate with `space-md`.
- **Bottom Navigation Clearance:** All scroll canvases mandate a static padding-bottom of `5.5rem` to avoid visual clashing with floating action bars or the fixed PWA bottom utility bar.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean chromatic separation and soft, organic ambient drop shadows rather than heavy structural borders.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** `#f8fafc`. Surface background containing all scrollable list views and collection activity streams.
- **Level 1 (Tonal Resting Cards):** Pure White (`#ffffff`) resting on Level 0, bound with an ultra-thin perimeter stroke (`1px solid #f1f5f9`) and a soft, emerald-tinted ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Sheets & Modals):** White containers raised with `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -5px rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Persistent Controls):** Floating Action Buttons (FABs) and fixed bottom navigation docks. Backdropped with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` when translucent, or anchored by `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a friendly, progressive geometry characterized by pronounced curved corners that echo natural organic forms.

- **Base Radii (`rounded-2xl` / `1rem`):** Standard actionable cards, operational summary panels, and input text containers.
- **Large Radii (`rounded-3xl` / `1.5rem`):** Hero metric cards, collection request overview modals, and full-width sheet containers.
- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full`):** Filter pills, status chips, progress bars, and high-priority primary trigger buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Buttons:** High-contrast pill (`rounded-full`) or `rounded-xl` structure with height fixed at 48px to 52px. Solid background in `#16a34a`, active state `#15803d`. Text in `#ffffff` via `label-lg`. Provide haptic visual response via subtle scale transform (`active:scale-[0.98]`).
- **Secondary Buttons:** Off-white background (`#f1f5f9`), border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, text in Deep Slate (`#0f172a`).
- **Icon Action Buttons:** Minimum tap box of 48x48px with centered 24px icon.

### Cards
- Surfaces rendered in `#ffffff` with `rounded-2xl` borders, enveloped by `1px solid #f1f5f9`.
- Card headers maintain an explicit horizontal layout: status indicator chip on the right, asset type / manifest ID on the left in Space Grotesk `headline-sm`.
- Impact cards (e.g., carbon offset, e-waste weight) feature a top subtle gradient edge line (`2px` high from `#15803d` to `#22c55e`).

### Input Fields
- Enclosed containers with height of 52px, `rounded-xl` borders, background `#ffffff`, and border `1.5px solid #e2e8f0`.
- Integrated leading icons (20px) styled in `#64748b` transitioning to `#16a34a` upon input focus.
- Focused state features a luminous border `1.5px solid #16a34a` with soft ring spread (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.15)`).

### Status Badges & Chips
- Fully rounded pills (`rounded-full`) with vertical padding of 4px and horizontal padding of 12px.
- **A Coletar / Success:** Background `#ecfdf5`, text `#15803d`, left-aligned dot marker in `#10b981`.
- **Triagem / Warning:** Background `#fffbeb`, text `#b45309`, left-aligned dot marker in `#f59e0b`.
- **Roteirizado / Info:** Background `#f0f9ff`, text `#0369a1`, left-aligned dot marker in `#0284c7`.

### Selection Controls (Checkbox & Radio)
- Square with soft corners (`rounded-md`, 6px) for checkboxes; circular (`rounded-full`) for radios.
- Unchecked: `2px solid #cbd5e1`, background `#ffffff`.
- Checked: `#16a34a` fill with pure white verification glyph. Touch surface padded to an invisible 44px boundary.

### Bottom Navigation Bar (PWA Dock)
- Fixed at the viewport bottom, protected by safe-area padding.
- Semi-translucent pure white background with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and top border `1px solid #f1f5f9`.
- Symmetrical layout of 4 to 5 routes: Collection Route, Scanner (QR/Barcode), Warehouse Triagem, Impact Dashboard, Settings. Active item uses `#16a34a` with an animated top dot accent.
